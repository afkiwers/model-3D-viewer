Try your own model

Open a STEP, IGES or BREP file from your computer to see how it looks, place markers on it and get the code for your own site. Click the pin with a plus in the toolbar, then click the model where a marker should go, and type its title and text straight into the card.

Your file stays on your computer. It is opened by your browser only; nothing is uploaded and no part of it leaves your device. You can also drop a file onto the viewer.

1Orientation

Models often arrive lying on their side or upside down. Turn yours until its front faces you and it stands upright (in the view cube, click the face that should be the front, then use the roll buttons), and press Make this the front view. The standard views, the view cube and the code below follow it.

Privacy

    
Visits to this page are counted with Plausible Analytics,
      which uses no cookies and collects no personal data. The models are shown entirely in your browser; nothing you do
      in the viewers is sent anywhere, and files you open with "Load local file" are never uploaded. The page asks GitHub for the number of the latest release.
